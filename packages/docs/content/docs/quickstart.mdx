## Chat apps: no install [#chat-apps]

For claude.ai, ChatGPT and Claude Desktop. Add Fortrip as a connector and sign in to Fortrip when your app asks. Steps: [claude.ai](/install#claude-ai) · [Claude Desktop](/install#claude-desktop) · [ChatGPT](/install#chatgpt).

## Coding agents [#coding-agents]

For Claude Code, Codex and other agents that run shell commands. You need Node.js <Fact path="CLI_NODE_MAJOR" /> or newer.

### 1. Install the skill [#install]

Run this once in your project. It asks which of your agents to add the skill to.

<pre><code><Fact path="SKILL_INSTALL_COMMAND" /></code></pre>

Prefer the Claude Code plugin or MCP, or use Cursor? [Set up your client](/install#clients) instead. Use one setup per agent.

### 2. Sign in [#sign-in]

```sh
npx fortrip login
```

The command opens your browser. Sign in to Fortrip, check that the code matches the one in your terminal, and approve. You can skip this step: the first time your agent needs access, it gives you a sign-in link.

## Ask your agent [#ask]

```text
Find a quiet hotel in Kyoto for two adults, two nights next month, with free cancellation.
```

You can ask it to find and compare hotels, check live prices and terms, book a stay, look up your bookings and cancel one under its terms.

## What to expect [#what-to-expect]

- **The agent asks you** for at least one guest's name per room, as on their passport, and a contact's name, email and phone. The hotel gets these to make the booking, and the confirmation goes to the contact's email.
- **Before you pay, you see** the total and its currency, the hotel, room and dates, the meals, the cancellation terms and any fees you pay at the hotel. The agent waits for your yes.

## Paying [#paying]

- Nothing is charged until you pay. You pay on Fortrip's payment page: the agent never sees your card.
- Prices are in the currency you ask for, USD (the default) or CNY, and you pay in it. A CNY price is paid through payment channels in mainland China. On a USD price, the page may offer to charge your card in its own currency at Stripe's rate, and Alipay and WeChat Pay show the total in yuan.
- On a USD price, a card is held until the hotel confirms, then charged. Alipay, WeChat Pay and every CNY payment are charged when you pay. If the hotel can't confirm, the hold is released or the payment refunded in full.
- The payment link is for you alone: anyone who has it can pay, so don't share it.

Questions about a booking, or a payment that didn't go through? See [Help and support](/faq#support).
