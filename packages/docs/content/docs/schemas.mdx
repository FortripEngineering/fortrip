The fields come from the zod schemas in the [`@fortrip/agent-contract`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fortrip/agent-contract) npm package.

## Reading the tables [#reading]

- Every field is present unless marked optional; `| null` means the field can be `null`. On catalogue facts `null` means not known, never no. Elsewhere it has its own meaning: `nextCursor` is `null` on the last page, and `payment.url` when there is no link to pay now.
- `[]` marks the items of a list, and "when `kind`" or "when `type`" a branch of a union, with that branch's fields below it.
- The schemas are open: [read responses tolerantly](/api#conventions).
- Field rules shared by every response (ids, [money](/api#money), dates, names) are in [Conventions](/api#conventions).
