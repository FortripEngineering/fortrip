## What you can do [#what-you-can-do]

- Search hotels in a city, region or country, near a place or point, or by name or brand, with an indicative price per room and night.
- Read a hotel's details: address, time zone, check-in times, the full description, its policies, amenities and rooms.
- Get live offers for up to <Fact path="LIMITS.offersHotels" /> hotels: the room, the meals, the total in USD, fees payable at the property, cancellation terms and how the booking is confirmed.
- Order an offer and have the user pay it; then follow the order, cancel it under its terms and list past orders.

Flights, activities and changes to existing orders aren't supported. The limits on stays and rooms are in [Errors & limits](/errors#limits).

## How an order works [#how-an-order-works]

1. **Search** for hotels. You get `hotelId`s and, when you give dates, indicative prices. See [How search works](/search).
2. **Get offers** for the hotels the user likes. Each offer has an `offerId` and expires at its `expiresAt`. See [the flow](/payments#flow).
3. **Order** the chosen offer with the guests and a contact. You get an unpaid order with its total and terms. If the price or terms changed, you get the changes instead. See [Order](/payments#order).
4. **Confirm** the total and terms with the user, then have them pay. See [Pay](/payments#pay).

## Key terms [#key-terms]

- **Offer**: a live, bookable room at a total price, with its terms. A search's **indicative price** only shows roughly what a hotel costs; it can't be booked.
- **Order**: what ordering an offer makes. It stays unpaid, and nothing is charged, until the user pays.
- **Payment link**: the order's `payment.url`, a Fortrip page where the user pays.
- **Link token**: a shared payment token from the user's Stripe Link wallet. Some agents can pay an order with one, once the user approves it.
- **Reference**: the order's number, starting `FT-`. The user quotes it to support.
