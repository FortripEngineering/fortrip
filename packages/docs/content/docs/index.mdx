## What you can do [#what-you-can-do]

- Search hotels in a city, region or country, near a place or point, or by name or brand, with an indicative price per room and night.
- Read a hotel's details: address, time zone, check-in times, the full description, its policies, amenities and rooms.
- Get live offers for up to <Fact path="LIMITS.offersHotels" /> hotels: the room, the meals, the total in USD, fees payable at the property, cancellation terms and how the booking is confirmed.
- Order an offer and have the user pay it by link, Link wallet token, or Alipay or WeChat Pay; then follow the order, cancel it under its terms and list past orders.

Offers and orders are for stays of 1 to <Fact path="LIMITS.nightsSupplier" /> nights, with up to <Fact path="LIMITS.rooms" /> rooms of the same party. Orders are charged in USD. <Fact path="GUIDE_SENTENCES.adaptivePricing" /> Prices can also be shown in <OtherDisplayCurrencies /> as a labelled estimate. Flights, activities and changes to existing orders are not supported.

## How an order works [#how-an-order-works]

1. **Search** for hotels and get `hotelId`s, with indicative prices when you give a stay. Indicative prices can't be booked.
2. **Get offers** for the hotels the user likes. Each offer has an `offerId` and expires at its `expiresAt`.
3. **Order** the chosen offer with the guests and a contact. You get an unpaid order: the total, the stay, its terms and the ways it can be paid. If the price or terms changed since the offer, you get `offer_changed` with every change instead.
