## Fees and terms [#fees]

- Calling the API, the MCP server and the CLI is free.
- There is no commission or revenue share for developers.
- Fortrip charges the guest the price shown.
- Fortrip's [terms](https://fortrip.ai/terms), [privacy policy](https://fortrip.ai/privacy) and [booking conditions](https://fortrip.ai/booking-conditions) apply.

## Testing your integration [#testing]

- There is no test environment. Any payment is real, and so is any cancellation penalty.
- Searching, reading offers and creating an order charge nothing. An unpaid order expires at its `payment.expiresAt`; to end it sooner, [close it](/payments#close).
- Each test order needs its own offer: an offer is ordered once.

## Your account [#account]

Create one at [fortrip.ai](https://fortrip.ai). Orders belong to the account that signed in when they were made, and so do their payment links and the [rate limits](/errors#rate-limits).

## Help and support [#support]

- **Paid, and still confirming.** The hotel is confirming the booking. Some hotels confirm at once and others take a while (the offer's `confirmation`). Ask your agent to check the order.
- **Failed.** No stay was booked. A card hold is released, or the payment refunded in full. The order's `failure.reason` says why.
- **Your bookings.** See them on [fortrip.ai](https://fortrip.ai), signed in to the account that made them (an order's `manageUrl` links there).
- **Anything else.** Email <Fact path="SUPPORT_EMAIL" /> with the booking number, which starts `FT-` (the order's `reference`), or, for an error, its `requestId`.
- **A security issue.** Email <Fact path="SUPPORT_EMAIL" />.

## Can my agent pay without the user? [#agent-pay]

No. The user confirms the total and the terms, then pays on the page the payment link opens. An agent can pay only with a [Link token](/payments#pay), which the user approves in their Stripe Link wallet for that one order.
