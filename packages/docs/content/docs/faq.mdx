## Fees and terms [#fees]

- Calling the API, the MCP server and the CLI is free.
- There is no commission or revenue share for developers.
- Fortrip charges the guest the price shown.
- Fortrip's [terms](https://fortrip.ai/terms), [privacy policy](https://fortrip.ai/privacy) and [booking conditions](https://fortrip.ai/booking-conditions) apply.

## Testing your integration [#testing]

- Search, hotel details and live offers are free reads.
- Creating an order charges nothing. An unpaid order expires at its `payment.expiresAt` and needs no cancelling.
- There is no test environment. Any payment is real, and so is any cancellation penalty.
- Repeated test orders of one stay hit `order_exists` while an order with the same guest names is open, and a stay takes at most <Fact path="LIMITS.ordersPerStay" /> orders.

## Your account [#account]

Create one at [fortrip.ai](https://fortrip.ai). Orders belong to the account that signed in when they were made, and so do their payment links and the [rate limits](/errors#rate-limits).

## Help and support [#support]

- **Paid, and still confirming.** The hotel is confirming the booking. The offer's `confirmation` says how long that takes: at once when `instant`, or within `withinMinutes` when `on_request`.
- **Failed.** No stay was booked. A card hold is released, or the payment refunded in full.
- **Your bookings.** See them on fortrip.ai, signed in to the account that made them. An order's `manageUrl` is its page there.
- **Anything else.** Email <Fact path="SUPPORT_EMAIL" /> with the order's `reference` (`FT-…`) or the error's `requestId`.
- **A security issue.** Email <Fact path="SUPPORT_EMAIL" />.

## Can my agent pay without the user? [#agent-pay]

No. The user confirms the total and the terms first. Then they pay themselves, on the payment link or with Alipay or WeChat Pay. A Link token, a shared payment token from the user's Stripe Link wallet, needs the user's approval in Link, and it pays one order only.
